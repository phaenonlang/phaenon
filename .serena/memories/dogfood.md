# Dogfood — the Tequila targets

Every milestone has a Tequila target, and a milestone is not done until the target's own test
suite passes on compiled output (`../design/roadmap.md` §3). The end goal behind it: tequila.dev —
about 50 repositories on Symfony, ext-mongodb, NATS and amp — running on Phaenon. That is why
ecosystem compatibility constrains every design choice.

Tequila repos have their own rules (`~/labs/tequila/platform/CLAUDE.md`): Conventional Commits,
squash-merged PRs, release-please. Do not apply Phaenon's commit style there, and do not merge or
push dogfood work without a maintainer's go-ahead.

  php-cs-fixer check, which skips files carrying `@generated by phaenon`.
- `../jetbrains/src/test/testData/reflector/*.phn` are copies of these sources.

- Its `tools/phpstan` is the PHPStan used for parity runs ([[testing]]).

## `tequila/core` — M4, started 2026-10-30

- Repo `~/labs/tequila/core` has the developer's own branches and worktrees checked out, so the
  dogfood lives in a **git worktree**: `~/labs/tequila/core-phaenon-dogfood`, branch
  `phaenon-dogfood` off `origin/main` at `8352ca6` (1.0.0-beta.8), `composer install` done.
  Local only, nothing committed there yet.
- The library is PHPStan **level 8** (`treatPhpDocTypesAsCertain: false`), 548 PHP files in
  `src/` + `tests/`; `phn check src tests` reads 810 files in about a second.
- First pass, plain PHP: 754 → **538 errors** after a day of checker fixes (`checker.md` §9,
  2026-10-30 bullets; fixture `internal/checker/testdata/tequila-core.phn`). Then **`phn migrate
  src tests`**: 810 `.phn` (the `.php` removed in the worktree, uncommitted), 252 docblock tags
  kept; the migrated tree checks at **502 errors**, 388 outside the tooling directories, 8 of them
  `P2516` — the PSR-3 `log($level, …)` parameter of anonymous loggers, which Phaenon wants spelled
  `mixed $level`.
  What is left, by kind: ~250 `P2103` and ~40 `P2312`, mostly level-9 strictness core never ran
  at (`mixed` into `string`/`array`/`resource`, `array` into `array<string, mixed>`), 13 of
  them `bytes` into `string` (the new overlay typing `random_bytes`, `hash(…, true)`,
  `Binary::getData()`); 73 `P2001` + 17 `P2002` + a dozen `P2006`/`P2005` in `tests/PHPStan`,
  `tests/Upgrade`, `src/PHPStan`, `src/Upgrade` — PHPStan's and Rector's own classes live in
  `tools/`, not in the root autoload, and are out of the dogfood's scope (116 findings); 66
  `P2202` return types, mostly `mixed`. Suspected checker gaps still to look at: the few
  `P2105 … on never`, `P2302`, `P2402`.
- Next: decide the exclusion of the tooling directories (an exclude option for `phn check`, or
  listing paths), work the remaining findings down (level-9 `mixed` strictness dominates), then
  `phn build` and the suite on compiled output.
